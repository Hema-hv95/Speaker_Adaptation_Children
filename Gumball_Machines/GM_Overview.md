Includes the gumball machine containing different ratios of blue gumballs to orange gumballs in different spatial arrangements: 
- 1 with 100 percent blue gumballs/0 percent orange gumballs  
- 1 with 0 percent blue gumballs/100 percent orange gumballs  
- 5 with 20 percent blue gumballs/80 percent orange gumballs  
- 5 with 40 percent blue gumballs/60 percent orange gumballs  
- 5 with 60 percent blue gumballs/40 percent orange gumballs  
- 5 with 80 percent blue gumballs/20 percent orange gumballs

All the ratios apart from 0 and 100 have their own folders as the ratios 20, 40, 60 and 80 have 5 versions each in differing spatial arrangements.  
The blue gumballs are concentrated 
- to the left: "-left"  
- to the right: "-right"  
- to the top: "-up"  
- to the bottom: "-down"  
- or scattered: "-scat"   

These were created to help control any biasing/undesirable effects of spatial orientation on perception.

The total number of gumballs is 30, and each ratio will produce whole number splits between blue and orange. 

The colours orange and blue were chosen for how easily distinguishable they are, even for people with red-green colour blindness.

How to read the file names: 
"20percentblue-left.jpg" - 20 percent of the gumballs are blue, and they are concentrated to the left of the gumball machine.

These images were created in Adobe Fresco, and other arrangements can be created using the images of the empty gumball machine (empty-gumballmachine.png), and the blue gumball (bluegumball-nobg.png) and the orange gumball (orangegumball-nobg.png) - the latter two of which are available as images with transparent backgrounds for ease of use. Colours can be modified by simply changing the hue or colour balance (while retaining the appearance otherwise).
