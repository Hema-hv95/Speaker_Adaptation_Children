**Test Phase \- Script**

*Participant clicks on the cartoon character Chloe to start the experiment.*

Chloe: Take a look at the gumball machine. (brief pause) Now, let's see what Sam has to say. / Let's look at the gumball machine again. (brief pause) Now, let's listen to Sam.  

Sam: I think (white noise) of the gumballs are blue/orange.  

*Image of a gumball machine containing a specific number of blue and orange gumballs is present on the screen. Chloe stands to the left of the gumball machine, and Sam stands to the right.*

Chloe:   
*If the ratio of gumballs is 0 or 100:*   
What do you think Sam said this time? Did he say "I think all of the gumballs are blue/orange" or did he say "I think none of the gumballs are blue/orange"? / What about this time? Do you think Sam said "I think all of the gumballs are blue/orange" or did he say "I think none of the gumballs are blue/orange"?    
*If the ratio of gumballs is 20, 40, 60 or 80:*  
What do you think Sam said this time? Did he say "I think many of the gumballs are blue/orange" or did he say "I think few of the gumballs are blue/orange?" / What about this time? Did Sam say "I think many of the gumballs are blue/orange" or did he say "I think few of the gumballs are blue/orange"?

*(Only for the first trial from the 0/100 category)*  
Chloe: If you think he said "all", press the square button. If you think he said "none", press the circle button. If you aren't sure, press the triangle button.

(*Only for the first trial from the 20/40/60/80 category)*  
Chloe: If you think he said "many", press the square button. If you think he said "few", press the circle button. If you aren't sure, press the triangle button.

*Participant clicks one of the three buttons (labelled "Many", "Few" and "Not sure" for the 20/40/60/80 category trials ; "All", "None", and "Not sure" for the 0/100 category trials).*

Chloe:   
*For the right answer:* That's right\! He did say / That's correct\! He did say / That's right\! he said  
*For the wrong answer:* That's not right…actually he said / That's not right…what he actually said was / That's not right…he actually said  
*For the "Not sure" button:*  I see\! Why don't we take a look at what he actually said?  
I see\! Well, this is what he actually said…

Sam: I think all/many/few/none of the gumballs are blue/orange.
