**Information about the Training Phase 1 Directory:**

Includes all the files pertaining to the first training phase of the experiment, including:  
\- Videos of the characters Chloe and Sam  
\- Images presented to the participant  
\- JavaScript code for use on the PCIbex farm  
\- CSV file used by the code to generate trials  
\- Script for the characters' responses and interactions  


Elements absent from this directory that are still used in the training phase trials include the green button animation and red button animation.   
The participant will select one of the buttons to indicate "yes" or "no", respectively.   
These video animations can be found in "Buttons" which is available as a separate folder in the main branch.  

**Information about Training Phase 1:**

Training Phase 1 is meant to prepare the participant for the tasks in the Exposure Phase, such that the Exposure Phase is able to achieve its purpose (of exposing the participant to speaker preferences) effectively. 

It contains an introduction and conclusion from the cartoon character Chloe to explain the task, and to provide instructions for the task.

It also contains five trials that follow the format of the trials/tasks in the exposure phase in that they also feature the cartoon character Sam, and an agreement-disagreement format. However, the images of the gumball machine are replaced by the images provided in the TP1_Images folder provided in the Training_Phase_1 directory. 
- 2 trials are attention/sanity check trials
  - 1 of these trials has "yes" for the right answer; the image used for this trial is "flowersvase.png".
  - 1 of these trials has "no" or the red button for the correct answer in order to eliminate any irrational aversion towards the red button; the image used for this trial is "caryellow.png".
- 3 trials are meant to prepare the participant to handle vague adjectives (similar to the vague quantifiers "few" and "many")
  - 1 of these trials triggers disagreement regardless of the button pressed/answer of the participant; the image used for this trial is "niceshoes.png".
  - the other 2 trials trigger agreeement regardless of the button pressed/answer of the participant; the images used for these trials are "manglasses.png" and "birdwings.png".

The goal is also to prevent the participant from perceiving Sam as someone who holds extreme or unconventional views or thresholds for vague adjectives, especially before the exposure phase. Thus, the definitive agreement cases of the vague adjective trials were structured so that Sam held the opinion of the vast majority with respect to the images, i.e., agreement was provided for the unremarkable/uncontroversial cases. The definitive disagreement case was structured such that it would not be abnormal for one to hold different opinions from another.
