**Training Phase 1 \- Script**

*Participant clicks on the video of Chloe to start the experiment.*

Chloe: Hi there\! I'm Chloe. 

Sam: Hello, I'm Sam.

Chloe: You and sam will be looking at some pictures now. Sam will ask you questions about them. To answer with a yes, you can press the green button. To answer with a no, you can press the red button. Sam may agree or disagree with your answers. Are you ready? Then let's begin\!

*Image of vase with flowers is shown on the screen.*   
Sam: Now take a look at this picture. Do you think there are flowers inside this vase?  
*Participant clicks yes.* Sam: I agree. I also think there are flowers inside this vase. (or)  
*Participant clicks no*. Sam: I disagree. I do think there are flowers inside this vase.

*Image of blue car is shown on the screen.*  
Sam: Here's another picture. Do you think this car is yellow?   
*Participant clicks yes.* Sam: I disagree. I don't think this car is yellow. (or)  
*Participant clicks no.* Sam: I agree. I also don't think this car is yellow.

*Image of a pair of regular red sneakers is shown on the screen.*  
Sam: Here's our next picture. Do you think these shoes look nice?  
*Participant clicks yes.* Sam: I disagree. I don't think these shoes look nice. (or)  
*Participant clicks no.* Sam: I disagree. I do think these shoes look nice.

*Image of a man wearing a pair of glasses that are large on his face.*   
Sam: Here's the next picture. This man is wearing glasses. Do you think his glasses are big?  
*Participant clicks yes.* Sam: I agree. I also think his glasses are quite big. (or)  
*Participant clicks no.* Sam: I agree. I also don't think his glasses are big.

*Image of a bird with its wings spread out.*  
Sam: Here's another picture. Do you think the wings of this bird are large?  
*Participant clicks yes.* Sam: I agree. I also think the bird's wings are large. (or)  
*Participant clicks no.* Sam: I agree. I also don't think the bird's wings are large.

Chloe: That was awesome \- great job\! Now, are you ready to do that a few more times? For the next few rounds, you and Sam will see pictures of a gumball machine \- like this \- with blue or orange gumballs. Once again, Sam will ask you questions about them, and you can press the green button to say yes, and the red button to say no. Are you ready? Let's begin\!
