This folder contains MP3 files of the sound effects that were incorporated into the animations of the buttons to convey that the participant's answer is correct or incorrect. The neutral sound effect is used for the animation of the triangle button in the test phase that represents the response "Not sure", i.e., other.

These sounds were collected from the following website: https://www.myinstants.com/en/index/us/ 

The "correct answer" sound effect is used with both the buttons of the exposure phase as we wish to avoid creating the impression that there are right or wrong answers to the participant. In fact, in doing so, we also wish to eliminate any bias with respect to the perceived "incorrectness" of the red button. 
The "incorrect answer" and "neutral" sound effects only make their appearance in the test phase to provide additional reinforcement to the participant.

They were chosen for the following reasons:
- they can be associated unambiguously with correctness or wrongness (or neither in the case of the "other" button)
- they sound playful enough to appeal to children
- they are neither too jarring nor too soft
