This folder contains images of the buttons used in the experiment.

The green ("yes") button and red ("no") button are used in the exposure phase of the experiment, and training phase 1.
The square ("All"/"Many") button, the circle ("None"/"Few") button, and triangle ("Other") button are used in the test phase of the experiment, and training phase 2.

The buttons are cartoonified to maintain the cartoon aesthetic adopted to appeal to the children involved in experimentation. Their colours were chosen to be easily distinguishable and easy to remember. 

These buttons are provided an impression of lighting up when pressed through the following steps:
- a glow effect is created on a layer separate from the one containing the image of the button
- in a screen recording of the button, the layer containing the glow effect (originally hidden) is displayed before being hidden again.
- these recordings, available as MP4 files under B_Videos, are then provided a sound effect (available under B_Sound_Effects) suited for their purpose (i.e., to convey that the answer is right or wrong)
- these MP4 files are embedded into the screen as selectors through the PCIBex code

The color and shape of the buttons can be modified as desired.
