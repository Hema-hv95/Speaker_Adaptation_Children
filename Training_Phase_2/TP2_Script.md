**Training Phase 2 \- Script**

*The cartoon characters Sam and Chris are displayed on the screen. The participant clicks on Chloe to begin the experiment and have her start talking.*

Chloe: Great work\! Now, we're going to look at some pictures of other things. This time, you'll be looking at the pictures with another friend of mine. His name is Chris. 

Chris: Hello, I'm Chris.

Chloe: Chris will describe the pictures one by one. But oh, we've moved to a noisy place. So, Can you help me find out what Chris is saying, when the noise blocks out his words? Then, let's give it a try\!

*The screen changes. There is an image of strawberries in a bowl printed onto the screen. Chloe stands to its left, and Chris stands to its left. There are three buttons printed below: the square one says "strawberries", the circle one says "blueberries", the triangle one says "not sure".* 

Chris: I think the (white noise) in this bowl taste nice.

Chloe: What do you think Chris said? Did he say "I think the strawberries in this bowl taste nice" or did he say "I think the blueberries in this bowl taste nice"? If you think he said strawberries, press the square button. If you think he said blueberries, press the circle button. If you aren't sure, press the triangle button.

*Participant responds with "strawberries".* Chloe: That's right\! He did say…  
*Participant responds with "blueberries".* Chloe: That's not right. Actually, he said…  
*Participant responds with "not sure".* Chloe: I see\! Well, what he actually said is…

Chris: I think the strawberries in this bowl taste nice.

Chloe: That was great, good job\! Now, are you ready to do that a few more times? For these next few rounds, you'll be looking at pictures of gumball machines again. Instead of Chris, Sam will be the one describing the pictures to you. Remember, we're all in a noisy place. So, when the noise blocks out Sam's words, can you think about how Sam might describe the picture, and then answer the question? Then, let's give it a try\!
