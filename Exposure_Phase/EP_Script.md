**Exposure Phase \- Script**

*Participant clicks on the video of Sam to start the experiment.*

Sam: Do you think (none/few/many/all) of the gumballs are (blue/orange)?

*An image of a gumball machine containing a specific number of blue and orange gumballs is present on the screen.*

*Participant clicks yes (the green button).* Sam: I agree. I (also) think none/few/many/all of the gumballs are blue/orange. (or) I disagree. I don't think none/few/many/all of the gumballs are blue/orange.

 *Participant clicks no (the red button).* Sam: I agree. I (also) don't think none/few/many/all of the gumballs are blue/orange. (or) I disagree. I (do) think none/few/many/all of the gumballs are blue/orange.

Note:  
These are Sam's intuitions/preferences with respect to the images of the gumball machine, depending on speaker conditions.

- Speaker Condition 1  
  - *Image has 0 blue gumballs, i.e., 100 percent orange gumballs*  
    - None of the gumballs are blue.  
    - All of the gumballs are orange.  
  - *Image has 20 percent blue gumballs, i.e., 80 percent orange gumballs*  
    - Few of the gumballs are blue.  
    - Many of the gumballs are orange.  
  - *Image has 40 percent blue gumballs, i.e., 60 percent orange gumballs*  
    - Many of the gumballs are blue.  
    - Many of the gumballs are orange.  
  - *Image has 60 percent blue gumballs, i.e., 40 percent orange gumballs*  
    - Many of the gumballs are blue.  
    - Many of the gumballs are orange.  
  - *Image has 100 percent blue gumballs, i.e., 0 orange gumballs*  
    - All of the gumballs are blue.  
    - None of the gumballs are orange.  
- Speaker Condition 2 (The only difference from Speaker Condition 1 is with the 40/60 ratios)  
  - *Image has 40 percent blue gumballs, i.e., 60 percent orange gumballs*  
    - Few of the gumballs are blue.  
    - Many of the gumballs are orange.   
  - *Image has 60 percent blue gumballs, i.e., 40 percent orange gumballs*  
    - Many of the gumballs are blue.  
    - Few of the gumballs are orange.
